Initial public release (v1.0.0):
- HTTPS or private-LAN HTTP with Android Keystore encryption, opt-in self-signed TLS, and Android 17 Local Network support.
- Interactive Dashboard: quick pause timers, stats time-range selector, scrubbable Query Activity & Upstreams charts, and clickable drill-downs.
- Query Log search, filters, detail sheet, and 1-tap Block/Unblock.
- Filters, custom rules, Blocked Services, Safe Browsing/Parental/Safe Search, and DNS Rewrites.
